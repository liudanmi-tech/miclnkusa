# Note: Assertiveness

## About This Skill
Use when: you struggle to say what you need, back down too easily under pressure, say yes when you mean no, feel invisible in conversations, or want to speak up more confidently without becoming aggressive.

---

## Baseline Fields

| Field | Label | Required | Description |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| `main_pattern` | Main Pattern | ✅ | Can't say no / back down when pushed / go quiet in groups / agree to keep the peace / indirect / over-explain |
| `key_contexts` | Key Contexts | ✅ | Work / relationships / family / social settings — where it shows up most |
| `root_cause` | What's Underneath | ✅ | Fear of conflict / fear of rejection / people-pleasing wiring / cultural conditioning / low self-worth |
| `current_situation` | Current Situation | ✅ | A specific situation that prompted this, or a general pattern you want to work on |
| `physical_response` | Body Response | ❌ | Whether assertiveness triggers physical symptoms — racing heart, blank mind, voice shaking |
| `desired_outcome` | What You Want | ❌ | Handle a specific situation / build a long-term habit / just understand the pattern |

---

## AI Onboarding Script

**Opening:**
> "Assertiveness is a skill, not a personality trait — it can be learned. Tell me about where this shows up for you."

**Questions (only for missing fields):**
1. `main_pattern` missing: "What does this look like for you specifically — saying yes when you mean no, going quiet when you have something to say, or backing down as soon as someone pushes back?"
2. `key_contexts` missing: "Where does it happen most — at work, with family, in relationships, or pretty much everywhere?"
3. `root_cause` missing: "What do you think is underneath it? Fear of conflict, not wanting to disappoint people, or something else?"
4. `current_situation` missing: "Is there a specific situation coming up where you need this, or is it more of a general pattern?"
5. `desired_outcome` missing: "What would success look like for you — handling one specific thing, or genuinely changing how you show up?"

---

## Sample baseline_text

```
Main pattern: Say yes to things I don't want to do, then resent it; go vague when I should be direct
Key contexts: Worst at work (with my manager) and with my mom
Root cause: Grew up in a family where expressing needs was seen as selfish; conflict felt dangerous
Current situation: Manager keeps adding to my workload without checking with me first — need to address it
Physical response: Chest tightens, mind goes blank when I'm put on the spot
Desired outcome: Handle the work situation + understand the pattern well enough to shift it over time
```

- New situation requires assertiveness
