# Note: DTR — Defining the Relationship

## About This Skill
Use when: you're ready to have the "what are we?" conversation, you want to move from casual to official, or you need to clarify expectations before things go further.

---

## Baseline Fields

| Field | Label | Required | Description |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| `context` | Relationship Context | ✅ | How long you've been seeing each other, what it currently looks like |
| `your_readiness` | Your Readiness | ✅ | Fully ready / nervous but ready / not sure what you want yet |
| `their_signals` | Their Signals | ✅ | Seems on the same page / hard to read / mentioned not wanting something serious |
| `fear` | Main Fear | ✅ | They say no / ruins the vibe / pushes them away / not sure how to bring it up |
| `what_you_want` | What You're Asking For | ✅ | Exclusive dating / official relationship / just clarity on where things stand |
| `timing_considered` | Timing | ❌ | Any specific moment or context you're thinking of for this conversation |

---

## AI Onboarding Script

**Opening:**
> "The DTR conversation is one of the most nerve-wracking things in dating — mostly because it feels high stakes, but usually it's more manageable than we think. Tell me where things are."

**Questions (only for missing fields):**
1. `context` missing: "How long have you been seeing each other, and what does it look like right now?"
2. `your_readiness` missing: "How do you feel about having the conversation — ready to just do it, or still on the fence?"
3. `their_signals` missing: "What signals are you getting from them? Do you think they're on a similar page?"
4. `fear` missing: "What's the thing you're most worried about with this conversation?"
5. `what_you_want` missing: "What specifically are you hoping to land on — just exclusivity, or an actual relationship label?"

---

## Sample baseline_text

```
Context: Seeing each other for 2 months, ~twice a week, have met some of each other's friends
My readiness: Ready — the ambiguity is starting to bother me more than the risk
Their signals: Seems invested, brought up future plans casually, hasn't mentioned seeing other people
Main fear: They'll feel pressured and pull back
What I want: Mutual exclusivity and an actual "we're together" acknowledgment
Timing: Thinking about bringing it up after dinner this Saturday
```

---

## Update Triggers

- Had the conversation — outcome to process
- Changed mind about what you want
- Their signals shifted
- Decided to wait — new timeline to consider
