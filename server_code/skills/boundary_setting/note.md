# Note: Setting Personal Boundaries

## About This Skill
Use when: you find it hard to say no in general, you feel drained by the demands others place on you, someone keeps crossing a line and you haven't been able to address it, or you're building the self-awareness to know what your limits actually are.

---

## Baseline Fields

| Field | Label | Required | Description |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| `boundary_type` | Type of Boundary | ✅ | Time / energy / emotional / physical / digital / financial / values-based |
| `who_with` | Who With | ✅ | Friend / partner / family / colleague / manager / general pattern across relationships |
| `current_pattern` | Current Pattern | ✅ | Say yes and resent it / feel guilty for having needs / over-explain when saying no / can't identify my own limits |
| `what_triggers_it` | What Triggers It | ✅ | Guilt / fear of conflict / fear of abandonment / need for approval / cultural conditioning |
| `specific_situation` | Specific Situation | ❌ | A current situation where a boundary is needed or has been crossed |
| `desired_outcome` | What You Want | ❌ | Set a specific boundary / understand your patterns / build long-term capacity |

---

## AI Onboarding Script

**Opening:**
> "Knowing what you need and being able to say so — without guilt or apology — is one of the most powerful skills there is. Tell me where you're struggling."

**Questions (only for missing fields):**
1. `boundary_type` missing: "What kind of limit is hardest for you — saying no to requests on your time, protecting your emotional energy, physical space, or something else?"
2. `who_with` missing: "Is this mostly with specific people, or a general pattern across different relationships?"
3. `current_pattern` missing: "What usually happens — do you say yes when you mean no, or do you say no but feel awful about it, or something else?"
4. `what_triggers_it` missing: "What's driving the difficulty? Is it guilt, fear of conflict, worry that saying no will damage the relationship?"
5. `specific_situation` missing: "Is there a specific situation or person where you need to set a limit right now?"

---

## Sample baseline_text

```
Boundary type: Time and emotional energy — I get pulled into other people's problems and can't get out
Who with: General pattern, but worst with two friends who treat me as their therapist
Current pattern: Can't bring myself to say "I can't talk right now" — end up on 2-hour calls I didn't want
Triggers: Deep fear of being seen as a bad friend; guilt if I'm not available
Specific situation: One friend calls almost daily in crisis; I'm starting to feel resentful and drained
Desired outcome: Learn to be a good friend without destroying myself in the process
```

---

## Update Triggers

- Set a specific limit and want to debrief how it went
- Relationship dynamic changes
- A new boundary is needed
- Understanding of your own patterns deepens
