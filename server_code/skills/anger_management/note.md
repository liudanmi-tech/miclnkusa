# Note: Anger Management

## About This Skill
Use when: anger is damaging your relationships or reputation, you say things you regret, you can't let go of perceived injustices, or you want to stop anger from running on autopilot and start using it constructively.

---

## Baseline Fields

| Field | Label | Required | Description |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| `anger_pattern` | Anger Pattern | ✅ | Explodes outward / turns inward (seething / withdrawing) / slow builds then erupts / physical tension |
| `main_triggers` | Main Triggers | ✅ | Disrespect / unfairness / feeling unheard / loss of control / specific person or situation |
| `key_contexts` | Key Contexts | ✅ | Work / relationships / family / traffic / online — where it shows up most |
| `impact` | Impact | ✅ | Has it damaged relationships, your professional reputation, or led to regret? |
| `current_situation` | Current Situation | ❌ | A specific ongoing situation or person driving the anger |
| `desired_outcome` | What You Want | ❌ | Stop a specific behavior / understand the pattern / respond better under pressure |

---

## AI Onboarding Script

**Opening:**
> "Anger gets a bad reputation, but it's not the problem — it's how it's expressed that matters. Tell me what's going on."

**Questions (only for missing fields):**
1. `anger_pattern` missing: "When anger shows up, what does it look like for you — do you explode outward, go quiet and seethe, or does it build slowly until something tips you over?"
2. `main_triggers` missing: "What tends to set it off? Feeling disrespected, something that feels unfair, losing control of a situation?"
3. `key_contexts` missing: "Where does it happen most — at work, at home, with specific people, or more broadly?"
4. `impact` missing: "Has it caused problems — things said you regret, relationships damaged, or your professional reputation affected?"
5. `current_situation` missing: "Is there a specific situation or person driving this right now?"

---

## Sample baseline_text

```
Anger pattern: Escalates quickly in confrontations — voice raises, says things I regret, then feels bad about it
Main triggers: Being interrupted or dismissed, especially by people I feel disrespected by
Key contexts: Work (one specific colleague) and at home with partner
Impact: Partner has said my reactions feel disproportionate; worried it's affecting how I'm seen at work
Current situation: Colleague keeps undermining me in meetings — last week I snapped at him in front of the team
Desired outcome: Stop the escalation cycle and handle the colleague situation without making it worse
```

---

## Update Triggers

- Anger episode occurred — want to debrief
- Specific situation resolved or worsened
- Pattern shifts (better regulation or new trigger)
- Impact on relationships changes
