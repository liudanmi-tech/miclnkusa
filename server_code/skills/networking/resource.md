## Core Frameworks

### The Informational Interview
The most underused networking tool for students. An informational interview is a 20-minute conversation where you ask someone about their career — not for a job.
- **Why it works**: Takes the pressure off both sides; people like talking about their work
- **How to get one**: A LinkedIn message or email with a clear, short, low-commitment ask
- **What to ask**: Their career path, what they look for in candidates, what they wish they'd known, what the day-to-day is like
- **What to do after**: Send a thank-you, stay in touch occasionally, and don't ghost them after getting advice

### LinkedIn Cold Outreach That Works
The message that gets replies is:
1. **Short** (under 80 words)
2. **Specific** (why them, not just "anyone in your industry")
3. **Low commitment** (a 20-minute call, not "can you help me find a job?")
4. **Student framing works in your favor**: Most professionals are willing to help students — your ask isn't weird, it's expected

### Career Fair Strategy
- **Before**: Research 5–6 companies you actually want to talk to — have a one-sentence pitch ready
- **During**: "I'm a [year] studying [major] and I'm really interested in [specific thing about their company] — can you tell me more about [specific role/team]?"
- **After**: Connect on LinkedIn within 24 hours with a note referencing the conversation

---

## Tactical Principles

1. **Give before you ask**: Like their posts, comment thoughtfully, engage first — then reach out
2. **Ask for insight, not favors**: "Can you tell me how you got into this field?" lands better than "Can you refer me?"
3. **Keep follow-ups short**: A brief check-in every few months ("I wanted to share that I just did X — your advice helped") maintains the connection without being needy
4. **Your school's alumni network is underused**: Most alumni will respond to a well-crafted note from a current student — they remember being where you are
5. **One good connection beats ten surface-level ones**: Invest in a few relationships rather than mass-messaging everyone

## Sample Scripts

**LinkedIn cold message to an alum:**
> Hi [Name], I'm a junior at [school] studying [major] and came across your profile while researching careers in [field]. I'd love to learn about your path from school to [their current role]. Would you be open to a quick 20-minute call sometime in the next few weeks? No worries if your schedule doesn't allow it — I appreciate you even reading this.
> [Your name]

**Post-career fair LinkedIn follow-up:**
> Hi [Name], great to meet you at the [company] booth at [school]'s career fair today. I really enjoyed hearing about [specific thing they mentioned]. I'd love to stay in touch and learn more about opportunities at [company]. Looking forward to connecting!

**Thank-you after an informational interview:**
> Hi [Name], I really appreciated the time you took to talk with me today. Your perspective on [specific thing they said] was genuinely helpful — it shifted how I'm thinking about [topic]. I'll definitely keep your advice in mind as I [specific next step]. I hope we can stay in touch as I move forward in this direction.

**Reconnecting after a few months:**
> Hi [Name], I wanted to reach out and share some news — I just finished [class/project/internship] and your advice about [something specific] was part of what pushed me in this direction. Hope things are going well on your end!
