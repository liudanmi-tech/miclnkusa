# Note: Imposter Syndrome

## About This Skill
Use when: you feel like a fraud despite evidence of your competence, fear being "found out," attribute your successes to luck rather than ability, or feel like you don't belong in a room you've earned your place in.

---

## Baseline Fields

| Field | Label | Required | Description |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| `context` | Where It Shows Up | ✅ | Work / school / new role / promotion / high-achieving environment |
| `main_thought_pattern` | Main Pattern | ✅ | "I got lucky" / "They'll find out I don't know enough" / "Everyone else gets it except me" / "I don't belong here" |
| `trigger` | Current Trigger | ✅ | New job / promotion / joining a new team / public visibility / a failure |
| `external_evidence` | External Evidence | ✅ | Objective indicators of competence (hiring, results, feedback) — often dismissed |
| `impact` | How It's Affecting You | ❌ | Holding back from opportunities / overworking to compensate / avoiding visibility / anxiety |
| `desired_outcome` | What You Want | ❌ | Manage a specific situation / understand the pattern / stop it from holding you back |

---

## AI Onboarding Script

**Opening:**
> "Imposter syndrome is extremely common among high-performing people — which is part of what makes it so frustrating. Tell me what's going on."

**Questions (only for missing fields):**
1. `context` missing: "Where is this showing up most — a new job, school, after a promotion, or somewhere specific?"
2. `main_thought_pattern` missing: "What does the inner voice actually say? Something like 'I got lucky,' 'they'll find out,' or 'I don't belong here'?"
3. `trigger` missing: "Was there a specific moment or change that activated this, or has it been there a while?"
4. `external_evidence` missing: "What would someone on the outside point to as evidence that you've actually earned your place — how did you get here?"
5. `impact` missing: "How is it actually showing up in your behavior — holding back, overworking, avoiding certain situations?"

---

## Sample baseline_text

```
Context: Just started a new role as a senior engineer at a bigger company — huge jump from previous job
Main pattern: "Everyone around me seems to intuitively get things I have to work hard to understand"
Trigger: First week — sat in a meeting and understood maybe 60% of the technical discussion
External evidence: Recruited by them, 4 rounds of rigorous interviews, 6 years of strong track record
Impact: Working 2–3 extra hours daily to "catch up," avoiding asking questions in meetings
Desired outcome: Stop the spiral before it tanks my confidence or burns me out in month one
```

---

## Update Triggers

- Specific fear was tested (spoke up, asked a question, didn't know something — what happened)
- Settled into the role, feelings shift
- New trigger (promotion, public recognition, failure)
- Pattern insight lands in a new way
