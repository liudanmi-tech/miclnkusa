# Privacy Policy

**MicLnk**
**Effective Date: May 5, 2025**
**Last Updated: May 5, 2025**

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to MicLnk ("we," "our," or "us"). MicLnk is a mobile application that helps you reflect on your daily experiences through voice recording and AI-powered analysis. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using MicLnk, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Password (stored in encrypted form)
- Optional profile information you choose to provide

### 2.2 Voice and Audio Data
MicLnk's core function is voice recording. When you use the app, we collect:
- Audio recordings you create within the app
- Transcripts generated from your recordings
- Duration and timestamp of recordings

**Important:** MicLnk is designed as a personal reflection tool. Our microphone permission is used exclusively for recordings you intentionally initiate by pressing the record button. We do not record in the background or without your active consent.

### 2.3 AI-Generated Content
We collect and store content created through our AI features, including:
- Scene images generated from your recordings
- Skill analysis results (Work, Life, and Mood categories)
- Summaries and insights derived from your recordings

### 2.4 Personal Profiles
If you choose to create profiles for people in your life, we collect:
- Names or nicknames you provide
- Relationship types you assign
- Optional profile photos you upload

### 2.5 Usage Data
We automatically collect certain technical information, including:
- Device type and operating system version
- App version
- Feature usage patterns (which features you interact with)
- Crash reports and error logs
- General interaction timestamps

### 2.6 Device Information
- Bluetooth device connection information (if you use a Bluetooth microphone)
- Device identifiers necessary for app functionality

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide core services:** Process your voice recordings, generate AI-powered images and skill analyses, and display your results within the app
- **Improve the app:** Analyze usage patterns to fix bugs and enhance features
- **Account management:** Authenticate your identity and maintain your account
- **Customer support:** Respond to your inquiries and resolve issues
- **Safety and security:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service

We do **not** use your personal information or recordings for advertising purposes. We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

To provide our services, we work with the following third-party providers:

### 4.1 Google Gemini API
We use Google's Gemini AI models to:
- Transcribe and analyze your voice recordings
- Generate scene images based on your recordings
- Provide skill analysis and insights

Your audio and text data is processed through Google's API in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Audio data is sent to Google's servers for processing and is not retained by Google beyond the duration of the API call, per their API terms.

### 4.2 Google Cloud Storage
Generated images and audio files are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. Google Cloud acts as a data processor on our behalf and is contractually required to maintain appropriate security standards.

### 4.3 Analytics
We may use privacy-preserving analytics tools to understand how users interact with the app. These tools do not receive personally identifiable information.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers located in the United States
- We implement industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest
- Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only
- We regularly review our security practices

Despite our efforts, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your account credentials confidential.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account data:** Retained for as long as your account is active
- **Voice recordings and transcripts:** Retained until you delete them or your account
- **Generated images:** Retained until you delete them or your account
- **Usage logs:** Retained for up to 12 months for analytics and security purposes

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain information.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

MicLnk is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the address below, and we will take steps to delete that information.

Users between the ages of 13 and 17 should review this policy with a parent or guardian before using the app.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

### California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)
If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- **Know** what personal information we collect about you
- **Delete** your personal information (subject to certain exceptions)
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information
- **Opt out** of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

### All Users
Regardless of your location, you may:
- Access and export your data through the app settings
- Delete your account and associated data
- Update your account information at any time

## 9. Microphone Access

MicLnk requires access to your device's microphone to record audio. This permission is used **only** when you actively choose to record. We do not access the microphone at any other time. You can revoke microphone access at any time through your device's Settings app, though this will prevent you from using recording features.

---

## 10. Bluetooth Devices

If you connect a Bluetooth microphone or recording device, MicLnk accesses Bluetooth solely to enable audio input from that device. We do not collect data about other Bluetooth devices in your vicinity.
