Privacy Policy

Chattoon  ·  Last Updated: May 12, 2026

Welcome to Chattoon, a mobile application developed by Chattoon ("we," "us," or "our"). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our voice journal and AI reflection service.

By using Chattoon, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored in encrypted form)
  • Optional profile information you choose to provide

1.2 Audio and Voice Data

Chattoon's core function is voice recording. When you use the app, we collect:

  • Audio recordings you create within the app
  • Transcripts generated from your recordings
  • Duration and timestamp of recordings

Important: Chattoon is designed as a personal reflection tool. Our microphone permission is used exclusively for recordings you intentionally initiate by pressing the record button. We do not record in the background or without your active consent.

1.2.1 Speaker Identification

When you create a profile for a person in your life, you may optionally provide a voice sample for that person. Chattoon uses this voice sample solely to identify who is speaking in your recordings (speaker identification). Specifically:

  • Voice samples you provide for profiles are stored on our servers and processed by Google Gemini AI to perform speaker matching within your recordings.
  • Speaker identification results are used only to label your personal transcripts and are never used for advertising, shared with third parties, or used to identify you outside of the app.
  • You can delete a voice sample at any time by editing or deleting the associated profile, and the sample will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Note: This feature processes voice characteristics that may constitute biometric data under applicable law. Use of this feature is entirely optional.

1.3 AI-Generated Content

We collect and store content created through our AI features, including:

  • Scene images generated from your recordings
  • Skill analysis results (Work, Life, and Mood categories)
  • Summaries and insights derived from your recordings

1.4 Personal Profiles

If you choose to create profiles for people in your life, we collect:

  • Names or nicknames you provide
  • Relationship types you assign
  • Optional profile photos you upload

1.5 Usage Data

We automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • General interaction timestamps

2. Use of Third-Party Services

2.1 Google Gemini API

Chattoon uses Google's Gemini AI models to transcribe and analyze your voice recordings, generate scene images, and provide skill analysis. Your audio and text data is processed through Google's API in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Audio data is sent to Google's servers for processing and is not retained by Google beyond the duration of the API call, per their API terms.

2.2 Google Cloud Storage

Generated images and audio files are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. Google Cloud acts as a data processor on our behalf and is contractually required to maintain appropriate security standards.

3. Content Safety and Moderation

As a provider of AI-generated content, we implement strict safety filters:

  • We utilize automated filters to block the generation of sexually explicit, hateful, or dangerous content.
  • Users are prohibited from attempting to generate content that violates intellectual property or local laws.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers located in the United States.
  • We implement industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Voice recordings and transcripts: Retained until you delete them or your account.
  • Generated images: Retained until you delete them or your account.
  • Usage logs: Retained for up to 12 months.

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

Chattoon is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information.

7. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access: You can request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to erasure: You can request that we erase your personal data.
  • Right to correct: You can request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to opt-out: We do not sell your personal data.
  • Non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending a notification through the app.
